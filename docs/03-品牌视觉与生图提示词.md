# 品牌视觉与生图提示词

> 用途：把「天枢 / Dubhe」的视觉资产交给生图模型时，保证**每张图是同一套东西**。
>
> 关键不是每个 prompt 写得多漂亮，而是**所有图共用同一段风格锚点**。
> 没有锚点，第 5 张图就会和第 1 张不是同一个产品。

---

## 1. 视觉锚点（唯一事实来源）

这些值不是拍脑袋定的，是从 `web/app.css` 的设计变量里取出来的。
界面和图片必须是同一套色，否则装到手机上会像两个产品。

### 色板

| 名称 | 色值 | 用在哪 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 紫 | `#6B5CFF` | 主品牌色，logo、主按钮 |
| 蓝 | `#3D9BFF` | 渐变终点 |
| 粉 | `#FF6FB5` | 氛围光，右下 |
| 薄荷 | `#3DDCC4` | 氛围光，左下 |
| 深底 | `#0A0A12` | 暗色底 |
| 浅底 | `#EEF1FA` | 亮色底 |

### 材质（Dubhe Glass）

界面上已经定死的三个特征，图片必须对得上：

1. **半透明磨砂玻璃**：模糊 32px、饱和度 180%、白色填充 52–74%
2. **四边厚度**：顶部受光面最亮、底部反光、左右侧壁半亮
3. **边缘色散**：边界泛出极淡的彩虹偏移（用量极小，多了变故障艺术）

### 光

主光从左上方来。界面里的高光会跟着指针走，静态图里表现为**左上亮、右下暗**。

## 2. 母提示词（Style Anchor）

**每一张图都要原样带上这一段。** 这是风格统一唯一可靠的保证。

```
STYLE ANCHOR — keep identical across the whole set:
Premium 3D render of liquid glass, Apple App Store icon aesthetic.
Palette: indigo #6B5CFF → azure #3D9BFF → magenta #FF6FB5 → mint #3DDCC4,
on deep space background #0A0A12 with a soft cyan bloom at the lower right.
Material: translucent frosted glass with heavy refraction, a thin bright rim light
along the upper-left edge, soft inner glow, and subtle chromatic dispersion
(rainbow fringe) along all edges. Key light from the upper-left, gentle backlight.
Rendering: physically-based glass, soft studio lighting, fine film grain,
no harsh contrast. Centered composition with generous negative space.
Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no signature.
```

## 3. 各资产提示词

把「母提示词 + 下面这段」拼起来发给 GPT 即可。

### A. App 启动图标（主图，最优先）

```
SUBJECT:
A luminous planet with a single tilted ring, floating in space.
The planet's core glows with a cyan-violet nebula texture seen through the glass,
like a miniature galaxy trapped inside a crystal sphere. The ring is made of the
same liquid glass, passing behind the planet at the upper-left and in front of it
at the lower-right. One tiny moon rests on the ring.

FRAMING:
Perfectly centered, the planet occupies about 62% of the frame width.
Generous even margins on all four sides — nothing touches the edge.
The background gradient must fill the entire square, no transparency at the borders.

SIZE: 1024x1024, square.
```

> 验收：缩到 48×48 还能认出「带环的行星」就算过。认不出就重画，不要将就。

### B. 暗色 / 亮色两个变体

暗色版就是 A 本身。亮色版把最后一句换成：

```
Replace the deep background with a soft pale background #EEF1FA.
Keep the glass material identical, but reduce the outer glow so the icon
still reads on a white home screen. The planet keeps its violet-cyan core.
```

> 验收：两版并排放在一起，必须一眼看出是同一个图标的两种底色，而不是两个图标。

### C. 品牌标识（纯 mark，给侧栏 / favicon / 网页用）

```
SUBJECT:
The same ringed planet, simplified into a flat vector-style mark.
Single continuous line weight, clean geometric ellipse ring, solid circular planet,
one small moon dot. Flat 2D, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no shadow.

FRAMING:
Centered in a square, the mark fills about 80% of the frame.
Solid indigo #6B5CFF on a plain white background.
Crisp edges suitable for downscaling to 24px.
```

> 这一张是给矢量化用的参考。真正上线用的是 `web/index.html` 里已经手绘好的
> `<symbol id="i-logo">`，出图只用来对照比例和气质。

### D. 启动页 / 首屏插画

```
SUBJECT:
A wide, calm scene: the ringed glass planet small and off-center to the right,
with faint orbital arcs sweeping across the frame and a few tiny glass shards
drifting in the foreground, heavily out of focus.

FRAMING:
Landscape 1536x1024. The left third must stay empty and quiet —
it will hold the product name and a loading indicator.
Depth of field: foreground shards blurred, planet sharp, background soft.
```

### E. 空态 / 关于页插画

```
SUBJECT:
A cluster of three small glass objects floating in a loose triangle:
the ringed planet, a translucent glass cube, and a glass sphere.
Each catches the light differently. Soft contact shadows below them.

FRAMING:
Square 1024x1024, objects occupying the center 70%, lots of air around them.
Background transparent.
```

## 4. 负面提示词（Negative Prompt）

每张图都带上，能省掉一半返工：

```
no text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no captions, no watermark,
no signature, no logo overlay, no UI elements, no buttons, no frames,
no borders, no drop shadow on the canvas edge, no harsh specular hotspots,
no plastic look, no matte finish, no flat shading, no clipart style,
no oversaturated neon, no lens flare, no bokeh balls
```

---

## 5. 统一性验收（每张图出完都要过一遍）

1. **缩到 48px 还认得出吗？** 认不出就重画。
2. **有没有字？** 生图模型很爱自己加标语，一律不要。
3. **有没有水印？** 平台水印不能带进产品。
4. **色板对得上吗？** 紫色偏红或偏蓝都算错，要 `#6B5CFF`。
5. **四边留白够吗？** 主体碰到边缘，套圆角遮罩时会被切。
6. **和上一张放一起是同一个产品吗？** 这条最重要。

---

## 6. 切图规格（出图之后）

### Android

自适应图标要**两层**，不能是一张图：

| 层 | 尺寸 | 内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 前景 `ic_launcher_foreground` | 432×432（108dp @4x） | 行星，**透明底**，主体只占中间 264×264 的安全区 |
| 背景 `ic_launcher_background` | 432×432 | 纯渐变，无主体 |

再补一张 `ic_launcher_round` 给圆形遮罩的设备。

### iOS / macOS

| 用途 | 尺寸 |
| --- | --- |
| App Store | 1024×1024（**不能有透明通道**） |
| iPhone @3x | 180×180 |
| iPad @2x | 152×152 |
| macOS | 1024 / 512 / 256 / 128 / 64 / 32 / 16 |

### 网页

`web/favicon.svg` 用矢量版（见 C），不要塞 PNG。

## 7. 参考图

`assets/branding/ref-icon-v1.png` 是这一版的方向参考：玻璃行星、环从前面绕过、
边缘有彩虹色散、深空底带青色辉光。**方向是对的**，但它有两个问题不能直接用：

1. 右下角有平台水印
2. 行星略偏右，四边留白不均

让 GPT 按上面的 prompt 重画时，把这张当 reference image 一起发过去，
并明确说：`match the material and lighting of the reference, but center it
and remove all text/watermark`。
